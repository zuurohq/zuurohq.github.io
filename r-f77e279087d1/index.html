What can go wrong

The profit rests on very few trades

The best 20 of 2,006 trades (1%) make 69% of the 3-year profit. The strategies, and the tested per-trade stops, were tuned on this same data. There is no daily or account stop.

3 micros is big for $1,000

A 100-point Nasdaq move is $600, 60% of the account. One day lost $665 (4 Mar 2026); counting open trades the balance fell to about $298 the session after.

It can go wrong fast

Using the 3-year pool, without weakening: of 100 simulated accounts about 3 have reached $0 or below after one month and about 9 after three. The first weeks are inside that window.

Tradovate can close your positions

With or without warning. Day margin is $100 per micro. The results here do not include close-outs or their $25/$50 fees.

Stops reach Tradovate only as messages

If a message is lost, that trade has no stop. GEN005 can hold overnight until its 4:40 pm flatten.

Futures losses can exceed your deposit

Use only money you can lose in full. The independent review rated this plan FAIL for money decisions, and it fails the project's own promotion gates. The live test is your decision and your risk.

The model is kinder than real life

Strategy choices and stop levels were picked on this same data, so real results will probably be worse. The what-if keeps trading down to $0; real margin rules would block or close a small account earlier. $600 triggers no action. Using the 3-year pool, without weakening: about 1 in 10 simulated first months fall at least $700 at a daily close, and an account that reaches $0 typically does so in 32 trading days.

ST13 is the least proven

+$3,080 on 119 trades from a 3-contract LAB-mode replay scaled to one contract (guard off), 81% of it in 2026. Operational one-contract behaviour is unverified, and the live permanent guard (about a $480 usable loss under its $800 limit) differs from this replay. Treat ST13 as unproven until it has traded live for a few months.

The three scripts cannot see each other

One signalled against another's open position 31 times in 3 years. The totals add independent histories, not the planned skip-opposite rule. MetaCopier says the broker nets MNQ per account; how one strategy's exit affects another's contract must be proven on the demo.

Margin, costs and close-outs

Day margin $100 per micro ($300 for 3). Overnight margin is $4,796.79 each, so Tradovate closes anything still open 15 minutes before 5 pm New York; NAVI flattens at 4:30 pm and GEN005 at 4:40 pm. Ahead of major news, intraday margin is 4 times normal and entries can be rejected. Costs about $0.94 per side (the simulation uses $0.95). Dated 7 Oct 2026 from Tradovate's pages: confirm in your account. Tradovate's own risk settings close all positions and lock trading. A liquidation email is not an early warning: it can arrive after the event.

Terms

What the words mean

Profit factor: gross winnings per $1 of gross losses; above 1 means profit.

Worst fall (max drawdown): the biggest fall from a high point to a later low.

Day margin: money the broker holds while a position is open.

Daily loss limit: a rule that closes everything after a loss of a set size; you chose not to use one.
