What the strategy must do
  • Reach $3,000 in 3 steps of $1,000.
  • Stay within a $2,000 trailing drawdown.
  • No single day above $1,500 (50% consistency).
  • One step per day. Flat by 4:15 pm New York.
What to be careful about
  • The latest year looks better than the two before it. Back-tests do not promise that continues.
  • Months swing a lot. Evals fail in clusters, so buying many at once adds less safety than it looks.
  • Whole history, including years older than these 3: 20% passed (282 of 1,433), profit factor about 1.0.
  • Fills use back-test prices. Real slippage can be worse.
How these numbers are counted
  • One Eval = one attempt, from start to pass or fail.
  • Only finished attempts count. An open one is left out.
  • A window counts attempts that started inside it.
  • Costs: $1.75 per side per contract was stress-tested elsewhere. Pass counts did not change.
