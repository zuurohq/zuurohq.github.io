MNQ Play for a $25K LucidFlex account: your plan in plain words

Trade the RangeProb-Tracks NQ calls on the micro contract (MNQ) toward a Lucid 25K Flex evaluation and payouts. One evaluation at a time. One funded account at a time. No copy trading.
Read this first. This is the $25K version. The $50K version of this plan was independently reviewed (verdict: FAIL as written, since corrected). This $25K page uses those corrections but has not been independently reviewed yet. The honest summary: the $25K account is roughly break-even. The payouts are small (at most $1,000 each) and the funded account starts with only 1 mini (10 micros), so the average gain is a few dollars per try and could be below zero. The $50K account looks better. Do not buy anything because of this page. Do the no-money practice run first.

Why $25K looks worse than $50K

The payout cap is half ($1,000 instead of $2,000) but the evaluation fee is not half ($65 instead of $105), and the funded account starts with 1 mini (10 micros). The $50K plan's best guess is about +$82 per try; this one is about +$9. If you want this play, the $50K account is the better use of the same rules.

Try the numbers yourself

Scale it up: more accounts at the same time

Your rule stays: one trade per call, one account per call, no copying. More accounts simply means each one takes a different call. Lucid allows up to 5 funded accounts and 10 accounts in total per household. Other firms with the same rules count as extra accounts. This only multiplies the same thin average: it does not make a break-even plan profitable.

What we tested (and what it does and does not prove)

We replayed about 6,000 past NQ calls (October 2023 to October 2026) on minute-by-minute prices with costs. Important: the vendor says calls before August 2026 were rebuilt by its engine on old prices, not live alerts. We compared against a "random buy/sell" version. That random version turned out to be flawed (it picked by the minute of the day, so it was mostly sells in a rising market), so "the calls beat a coin flip" is not proven. A fair re-test is needed.

Two pre-agreed tests failed: "the help shows up in every period" (it did not in 2023-24) and "the first level wins often enough to pay after costs" (57.8% against about 60% needed). The second level was picked after looking at results, so treat it as exploratory, not proven. Standalone, the second-level trades earned about break-even over three years.

What can go wrong

The edge fades or never existed. It was absent in 2023-24 and the comparison was flawed.
It has never been traded live. Results are replays and computer estimates. We have not yet replayed the 4:45 pm close or the funded contract limits, and the corrected estimate is lower because of them.
About 3 in 4 evaluations fail. Plan your budget before you start.
Calls must reach you fast. The replay assumes you can enter the minute a call fires. Late alerts mean worse prices.
The vendor's numbers are not audited. Their 80% win rate does not mean 80% profit; we measured the real thing ourselves.
Costs. Lucid lists $0.50 per side for MNQ; membership ($100 a month) and any data fees are extra.
