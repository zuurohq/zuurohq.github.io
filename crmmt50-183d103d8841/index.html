What changed and what is not shown

  • Eval figures from 15 Oct are withdrawn. That run was made before the final v1.4 script was saved: only 2,215 of its 8,086 trades match this run. This run was repeated and gave identical summary figures.
  • Funded figures are consistent with 15 Oct: 9,576 of 9,580 earlier trades are identical, and 21 trades are new.
  • Pass rates are not shown. The 50K builds carry no attempt counter and the replay has not been re-run on the new lists. Earlier replay rates are retired with the v0.8 and v1.0 sections.
  • The last 365 days carry the Eval result. Eval net over the last 365 days exceeds its entire-history net, so everything before that period nets slightly negative (the difference is about −$653). Treat recent windows as one regime.
  • The recent two years carry the result. Net over the last two Sep–Aug years exceeds the entire-history net for Eval, so older years are net negative. Treat recent windows as one regime, not a proven edge.
  • In-sample. Settings were chosen on these years. Live fills are not matched to these lists.
  • Symbols. Both v1.4 builds are NQ1! builds. The older MNQ1! 5-contract Funded (v0.8) is retired and not shown.
