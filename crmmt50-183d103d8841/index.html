Reading these numbers

  • Eval: 3 legs of +$1,000 (last leg tops up to $3,000 plus $50 cost buffer), no day above $1,500 profit, trailing loss $2,000 from each leg's peak, one leg per trading day, flat by 16:15 ET. A new attempt starts the next trading day after a pass or a fail.
  • Funded: 5 legs (first +$3,740, then +$165 four times), trailing loss $2,000 from each leg's peak, one leg per trading day, 25 point emergency stop.
  • Costs on: $1 per side per contract plus the default slippage of the saved scripts. In-sample: settings were chosen on this history.
  • Eval to payout follows each Eval pass into the Funded attempt that starts next, and counts it when that attempt reaches its first payout leg (+$3,740). Funded runs are a separate back-test, so this chain is an estimate and small windows hold very few passes.
  • Funded stage pass rate counts attempts that complete all 5 legs; the first payout rate is shown in the chain.
